Projects

Energy portfolio optimisation

Risk-based allocation across renewable and fossil fuel assets using modern portfolio theory, with a GARCH-HMM volatility measure and Monte Carlo simulation over a synthetic €10,000 book.

Python · NumPy · Monte Carlo · GARCH

Coral bleaching — Potts model

Markov chain simulation of temperature-induced reef degradation and recovery. Parameters trained against observed bleaching at Scott Reef, Western Australia, March 2010, with spatial correlation and phase transition analysis.

Python · scikit-learn · Potts model · Seaborn

HMM-GARCH volatility regimes

Detecting volatility and phase changes in high-frequency time series by feeding GARCH values in as emission probabilities for a hidden Markov model, with random matrix theory for signal processing. Validated against ten years of oil market data by checking regime shifts coincide with known geopolitical shocks.

Python · GARCH · HMM · random matrix theory

3D PDE solvers

Numerical solutions to partial differential equations by successive over-relaxation, visualised in three dimensions across varying boundary conditions. Covers heat diffusion, wave propagation, Schrödinger and Black-Scholes.

Python · C++ · SOR · finite difference
