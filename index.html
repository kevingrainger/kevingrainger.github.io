Kevin Grainger

I'm a quantitative analyst in London working on climate physical risk. The projects below sit across three themes: earth systems, signal processing, and energy markets. Some grow out of expertise built in my current role, some are passion projects, and some began as numerical and mathematical physics simulations from my undergraduate degree in theoretical physics. Most start from someone else's published model and try to add one honest result.
