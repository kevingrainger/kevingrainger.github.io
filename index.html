Projects

Coral reef tipping points — Potts model

A lattice model of Scott Reef, Western Australia, calibrated to 23 years of coral survey data and satellite heat stress (degree heating weeks from NOAA sea-surface temperature). Tests whether the tipping points and hysteresis seen in mean-field reef models survive when interactions are local, looking at cusp surfaces, transition order and early warning signals.

Python · Potts model · Approximate Bayesian Computation · Google Earth Engine

Olive grove Xylella — stochastic SIR model

A recreation of A lattice model to simulate the vector and the infection of the Xylella fastidiosa on olive trees by Annalisa Fierro, Antonella Liccardo and Francesco Porcelli (Scientific Reports, 2019). Their model has spittlebugs moving across a grove lattice and spreading Xylella between trees by stochastic SIR transmission.

My extension examines the simulation as a percolation process, following Grassberger (1983) and Newman (2002). Attempting to predict the final outbreak size analytically. A separate front-speed argument predicts the timeline, including the annual staircase pattern. Both are checked against the simulation.

Stochastic calculus · agent-based modelling · percolation theory · SIR

corrlib — correlation toolbox

A Python library for my personal projects: tail dependence versus correlation, correlation at high sampling frequency, cleaning noisy correlation matrices with random matrix theory, and minimum-variance allocation. Also contains: stack module combining many noisy sensors into one signal, using the same formula as minimum-variance.

Signal processing · NumPy · random matrix theory · yfinance

Seismic denoiser

Earthquake detection in noisy seismometer records. Compares an various detection algorithms against STA/LTA, the field's standard baseline, then adds array stacking from simple averaging up to optimal weights. Scored on signal-to-noise gain, pick-timing error and the smallest magnitude detected.

Seismology · signal processing · correaltion cleaning

Light-weight Option pricer: European, American, Asian

Black–Scholes with time-varying volatility σ(t), capturing seasonal and near-expiry volatility patterns. European options validate the solver against the exact formula and are then calibrated to SPX and SET50 (Bangkok) chains. American options use projected SOR from my PDE solver project; Asian options use the Večeř reduction, cross-checked by Monte Carlo.

PDE solvers · finite difference · projected SOR · Monte Carlo

Italian power price volatility

Replicates Guerzoni, Riso & Zoia (2026) on how weather drives Italian electricity price volatility, then replaces observed weather with bias-corrected SEAS5 seasonal forecasts. Tests whether the forecast-driven model beats the hindsight version. Aimed at use for energy hedging.

Energy modelling · GARCH-MIDAS · ERA5 · SEAS5

Spatially correalted portfolio managment: Spanish solar portfolio

How output from solar sites across Spain moves together, estimated from ERA5 and cleaned with corrlib, combined with ENTSO-E prices and capture rates. Builds an allocation strategy with rebalancing rules and risk limits, and tests its robustness across parameter choices.

Solar · ERA5 · ENTSO-E · corrlib
